NAME
    sound - the sound card

SYNOPSIS
    sound
    sound test
    sound volume <0-100>

DESCRIPTION
    MOOS plays sound on UEFI PCs through two kinds of sound card:

        Intel High Definition Audio   almost every PC since about 2005,
                                      whatever the codec chip on it
                                      (Realtek, Conexant, IDT, ...)
        AC'97                         older PCs and some virtual
                                      machines

    The card is set up the first time something wants to play. For HD
    Audio, MOOS looks at the codec's parts and follows the wires from
    an output jack (line out, speaker or headphones) back to a
    converter, and turns everything on that path up and on.

    Sound is played at 44.1 or 48 kHz, 16-bit stereo; other rates are
    converted. The browser's video and audio player uses it.

    sound            what MOOS found: the card, the codec, the output
                     it plays to, the rates and the volume
    sound test       plays a 440 Hz tone for one second, then 880 Hz,
                     and says how much the card played
    sound volume N   sets the volume, 0 to 100 (the card's amplifier)

NOTES
    Sound needs a UEFI PC: the drivers are part of the full MOOS. On a
    BIOS PC the command says so.

    MOOS uses no interrupts for sound; it keeps the card fed from the
    desktop's main loop and clears what has been played, so a pause
    never repeats old sound.

    In QEMU, add -device intel-hda -device hda-output (or -device AC97)
    to hear it; tools/screentest.py --audio FILE records it to a .wav.
