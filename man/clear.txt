NAME
    clear - clear the screen

SYNOPSIS
    clear

DESCRIPTION
    clear wipes everything off the screen and puts the prompt back in
    the top-left corner. Nothing else changes: your files, your command
    history and your colour settings stay exactly as they were.

    The status bar at the bottom of the screen is never cleared.

ARGUMENTS
    None.

EXAMPLES
    clear
    cls          the same, using the DOS-style alias

SHORTCUT
    Ctrl+L clears the screen at any time while you are typing at the
    prompt, without losing what you have typed so far.

ALIASES
    cls
