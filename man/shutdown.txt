NAME
    shutdown - turn the computer off

SYNOPSIS
    shutdown

DESCRIPTION
    shutdown ends MOOS and switches the computer off. Your files are
    already safe on the disk - MOOS saves every change immediately -
    so it is fine to shut down at any time.

    MOOS switches the power off the standard way: it looks up the
    power controls in the computer's ACPI tables and uses them. This
    works on practically every PC made in the last 20 years and in
    virtual machines (QEMU, VirtualBox, VMware, Hyper-V), whose window
    then closes. On UEFI PCs MOOS can also ask the firmware to power
    off.

    If none of that works (very old PCs), MOOS stops safely and shows:

        It is now safe to turn off your computer.

    Then simply press the power button.

ARGUMENTS
    None.

EXAMPLES
    shutdown
    exit         alias
    poweroff     alias
    halt         alias

ALIASES
    exit, poweroff, halt

SEE ALSO
    reboot
